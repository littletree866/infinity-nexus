Welcome to Part 8 of the Pokémon Infinite Sun Walkthrough! After obtaining the Poké Flute from Mr. Fuji, we can finally enter Saffron City. However, Team Rocket has taken over the Silph Co. building. We'll need to liberate the company before we can challenge Sabrina at the Saffron Gym.

Saffron City

Saffron City

Saffron City is the largest and most central city in the Kanto region. It features the Silph Co. headquarters, the Fighting Dojo, and the Saffron Gym. Currently, Team Rocket is occupying the Silph Co. building and has locked down the city's gates.

Fighting Dojo

The Fighting Dojo is located just west of the Saffron Gym. If you defeat the Blackbelt inside, he will offer you a choice between two Fighting-type Pokémon: Hitmonlee or Hitmonchan.

Mr. Psychic's House

In the house to the south, a man named Mr. Psychic will give you TM29 Psychic if you have an empty slot in your party.

Copycat's House

A little girl in the house to the east has lost her favorite Pokémon doll. You can find a replacement in Vermilion City (using the Itemfinder near the S.S. Anne dock). She will reward you with TM41 Torment and a Magnet Train Pass (in some versions of the game).

Silph Co. Takeover

Silph Co.

The Silph Co. building is a massive 11-floor labyrinth filled with Team Rocket Grunts and teleporters. Your goal is to reach the top floor, defeat Giovanni, and rescue the company president. You can find several useful items along the way, including a Master Ball.

Silph Co. Trainers

The building is crawling with Team Rocket Grunts. You'll also encounter a few scientists and other trainers who have been trapped inside.

Boss Giovanni

After reaching the 11th floor and defeating the remaining grunts, you'll face Giovanni, the leader of Team Rocket. He uses Ground and Normal-type Pokémon. Water, Grass, and Ice moves will work well here.

After defeating Giovanni, he will flee, and the Silph Co. president will reward you with the Master Ball. You are now free to challenge the Saffron Gym.

Saffron Gym (Sabrina)

Saffron Gym

The Saffron Gym specializes in Psychic-type Pokémon. The Gym is a maze of teleporters. To reach Sabrina, you must navigate the correct sequence of warp tiles. Psychic-types are weak to Bug, Ghost, and Dark moves, so bring your best counters.

Gym Trainers

Before challenging Sabrina, you'll face several Psychic trainers. They use Pokémon like Slowpoke, Mr. Mime, and Kadabra.

Gym Leader Sabrina

Sabrina uses three Psychic-type Pokémon: Kadabra, Mr. Mime, and Alakazam. Alakazam is incredibly fast and has a high Special Attack, so it can hit hard with Psybeam and Psychic. Use physical attacks (like Normal or Fighting moves) if you can, as her Pokémon have low Defense.

Defeating Sabrina earns you the Marsh Badge, which makes all Pokémon up to level 70 obey you. She will also give you TM04 Calm Mind.
