Welcome to Part 2 of the Pokémon Infinite Sun Walkthrough! In this section, we will navigate through the treacherous Viridian Forest, explore Pewter City, and take on our first Gym Leader, Brock.

Viridian Forest

Viridian Forest

Viridian Forest is a maze-like woodland located between Viridian City and Pewter City. It is famous for being the first major obstacle for trainers, filled with Bug-type Pokémon and trainers looking to test their skills.

Before entering, make sure you have plenty of Potions and Antidotes, as Poison-type moves are common here.

Navigation

The forest is a bit of a maze. Follow the path north, battling the trainers along the way. You'll eventually find a clearing with tall grass where Pikachu can be found. Keep heading north and east to find the exit.

Trainers

There are several Bug Catchers in the forest. They use low-level Bug Pokémon, making them great for training your starter or any Flying-type you may have caught.

Pewter City

Pewter City

Pewter City is a city made of stone, nestled between Viridian Forest and Mt. Moon. It is home to the Pewter Museum of Science and the first Gym in the region.

Poké Mart

Before challenging the Gym, stock up on Potions. Brock's Pokémon are tough Rock-types, so you might want to buy an Escape Rope if you plan on exploring Mt. Moon soon.

Pewter Gym

Pewter Gym

The Pewter Gym specializes in Rock-type Pokémon. If you chose Charmander, this will be a tough battle unless you caught a Mankey or a Grass-type in Viridian Forest. If you chose Squirtle or Bulbasaur, you have a major type advantage.

Gym Trainers

Before you can challenge Brock, you must defeat the Gym Trainer. He uses Rock-type Pokémon, so bring your best Water or Grass moves.

Gym Leader Brock

Brock uses two Rock-type Pokémon. His Geodude is straightforward, but his Onix has high defense and can be annoying to take down if you don't have a special attacker or a type advantage. Avoid using Electric, Fire, Flying, or Bug-types here.

Defeating Brock earns you the Boulder Badge, which makes all Pokémon up to level 21 obey you, and allows the use of Flash outside of battle. He will also give you TM39 Rock Tomb.
