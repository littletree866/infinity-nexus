Welcome to Part 5 of the Pokémon Infinite Sun Walkthrough! In this section, we'll travel south from Cerulean City through Routes 5 and 6 to Vermilion City, explore the luxurious S.S. Anne, and take on the Vermilion Gym to earn the Thunder Badge.

Route 5

Route 5

Route 5 connects Cerulean City to Saffron City, though the main gate to Saffron is currently locked. The route features a Day Care Center where you can leave Pokémon to gain experience, and a path leading south to Route 6.

Route 6

Route 6

Route 6 is a short path that leads directly into Vermilion City. You'll encounter a few trainers here, and it's a good place to catch some Water and Grass types before taking on the Gym.

#Point of Interest
1Path to Route 5 (North)
2Path to Vermilion City (South)

Trainers

A couple of trainers stand between you and Vermilion City. They use a mix of Normal and Ground types.

Vermilion City

Vermilion City

Vermilion City is a bustling port town. It features a Pokémon Center, a Poké Mart, a Pokémon Fan Club, and the Vermilion Gym. The S.S. Anne is currently docked at the harbor, and you can access it with the S.S. Ticket from Bill.

S.S. Anne

Show the S.S. Ticket to the guard at the harbor to board the ship. Inside, you'll find a luxurious cruise liner filled with trainers to battle. Your goal is to reach the Captain's quarters at the back of the ship. Along the way, you can find several hidden items, including a Rare Candy. After helping the Captain, he will give you HM01 Cut.

Vermilion Gym

Vermilion Gym

The Vermilion Gym specializes in Electric-type Pokémon. The Gym features a puzzle where you must find two hidden switches in the trash cans to open the path to the Leader. Avoid using Water or Flying-types here—they will be at a major disadvantage.

Gym Trainers

Before challenging Lt. Surge, you must defeat a Gym Trainer. He uses Electric-types, so bring your best Ground-type moves.

Gym Leader Lt. Surge

Lt. Surge uses two Electric-type Pokémon: Voltorb and Raichu. Raichu is fast and can hit hard with Thunderbolt. Use Ground-type moves to make this battle much easier, as Electric moves will have no effect on Ground-types.

Defeating Lt. Surge earns you the Thunder Badge, which makes all Pokémon up to level 40 obey you, and allows the use of Fly outside of battle. He will also give you TM34 Shock Wave.
