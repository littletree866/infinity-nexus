Welcome to Part 3 of the Pokémon Infinite Sun Walkthrough! After defeating Brock, the journey continues east through Route 3, deep into the cavernous Mt. Moon, and finally into Cerulean City.

Route 3

Route 3

Route 3 is a winding path that leads from Pewter City to the entrance of Mt. Moon. You'll encounter several trainers here who are also heading toward the mountain. This is a great place to train your team before the cave.

#Point of Interest
1Path to Pewter City (West)
2Pokémon Center (Near Mt. Moon)
3Mt. Moon Entrance (East)

Trainers

There are several trainers on this route. Make sure your team is healed up before tackling them.

Mt. Moon

Mt. Moon

Mt. Moon is a large cave that connects Route 3 to Route 4. It is famous for its supply of Moon Stones and the fossilized Pokémon found within. The cave is filled with Zubat and Geodude, so bring plenty of Potions and Repels.

#Point of Interest
1Entrance (From Route 3)
2Fossil Room (Choose Helix or Dome)
3Exit (To Route 4)

Trainers

Mt. Moon is crawling with Team Rocket grunts and other trainers looking to cause trouble.

Route 4

Route 4

Route 4 is a short route that connects Mt. Moon to Cerulean City. It also has a small path leading to the entrance of the Cerulean Cave, which is currently blocked off. You will find a Move Tutor here in some versions of the game, but for now, just head east to Cerulean.

#Point of Interest
1Exit from Mt. Moon (West)
2Path to Cerulean City (East)

Cerulean City

Cerulean City

Cerulean City is a beautiful city surrounded by water. It is home to the second Gym, led by Misty, a Water-type specialist. The city also features a Bike Shop, a Pokémon Center, and a Poké Mart.

#Point of Interest
1Pokémon Center
2Poké Mart
3Cerulean Gym
4Bike Shop
5Cerulean Cave Entrance (Blocked)
6Path to Route 24 (North)
7Path to Route 5 (South)

Poké Mart

Stock up on Super Potions and Repels before heading out to Routes 24 and 25, or challenging the Gym.
