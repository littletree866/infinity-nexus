Welcome to Part 4 of the Pokémon Infinite Sun Walkthrough! Now that you've arrived in Cerulean City, it's time to explore the routes to the north and take on your second Gym Leader, Misty.

Route 24 (Nugget Bridge)

Route 24

Route 24 is famous for the Nugget Bridge, a challenge where you must defeat five trainers in a row to win a Nugget. It is also a great place to catch some new Pokémon, including Abra and Bellsprout.

#Point of Interest
1Nugget Bridge
2Path to Route 25 (North)
3Path to Cerulean City (South)

Nugget Bridge Trainers

You must defeat all five trainers on the bridge in a row. If you win, the last trainer gives you a Nugget. But beware—afterward, he reveals himself to be a Team Rocket Grunt and tries to recruit you! Refuse, and defeat him.

Route 25 & Bill's Cottage

Route 25

Route 25 is a peaceful path leading to Bill's Cottage. You'll find several trainers here, including a few who are training their Pokémon. At the end of the route, you'll meet Bill, a famous Pokémon Researcher who has gotten himself into a bit of a predicament.

#Point of Interest
1Path to Route 24 (South)
2Bill's Cottage (North)

Trainers

The trainers here are slightly stronger than those on Route 24. Make sure your team is healed up.

Bill's Cottage

At the end of the route, you'll find a cottage. Inside, a Pokémon is running around a teleporter machine. Talk to it, and you'll discover it's actually Bill, fused with a Clefairy! Help him by using his computer to separate them. Afterward, he'll give you the S.S. Ticket, granting you access to the S.S. Anne in Vermilion City.

Cerulean Gym

Cerulean Gym

The Cerulean Gym specializes in Water-type Pokémon. If you chose Charmander, this will be a tough battle. If you chose Bulbasaur or Squirtle, you should have an easier time. Avoid using Fire, Ground, or Rock-types here.

Gym Trainers

Before challenging Misty, you must defeat two Gym Trainers. They use Water-type Pokémon, so bring your best Grass or Electric moves.

Gym Leader Misty

Misty uses two Water-type Pokémon: Staryu and Starmie. Starmie is fast and has a high Special Attack, so it can deal serious damage with Water Gun and Swift. Use Grass or Electric moves to take it down quickly.

Defeating Misty earns you the Cascade Badge, which makes all Pokémon up to level 30 obey you, and allows the use of Cut outside of battle. She will also give you TM03 Water Pulse.
