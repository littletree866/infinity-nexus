Welcome to Part 6 of the Pokémon Infinite Sun Walkthrough! In this section, we'll traverse Route 7 to reach the bustling Celadon City, take on the Celadon Gym to earn the Rainbow Badge, and infiltrate the secret Team Rocket Hideout beneath the Game Corner.

Route 7

Route 7

Route 7 is a short path connecting Saffron City to Celadon City. The gate to Saffron City is locked for now, so just head straight east toward Celadon. You'll find a few trainers on the way and some decent Pokémon to catch.

Trainers

A couple of trainers are waiting on this short route. They use mid-level Pokémon, so be prepared.

Celadon City

Celadon City

Celadon City is the largest city in Kanto, known for its massive Department Store and the Game Corner. It is also home to the Celadon Gym, led by Erika, a Grass-type specialist. The city has a lot to explore, including the Celadon Condominiums and a man who will give you a free Eevee.

Celadon Department Store

The Department Store is the biggest shop in the game. You can buy TMs, evolution stones, and a variety of Poké Balls here. Be sure to check out the rooftop for vending machines and a thirsty girl who will give you TMs in exchange for drinks.

Game Corner & Rocket Hideout

The Game Corner is a casino where you can play slots to win coins for rare Pokémon. However, there is a suspicious poster on the wall. If you interact with it, you'll discover a hidden switch that opens the entrance to the Team Rocket Hideout.

Team Rocket Hideout

The hideout is a maze filled with Team Rocket grunts. Your goal is to reach the bottom floor, defeat the boss, and retrieve the Silph Scope. The Silph Scope is required to identify the ghost in the Pokémon Tower in Lavender Town.

Celadon Gym (Erika)

Celadon Gym

The Celadon Gym specializes in Grass-type Pokémon. The Gym is filled with beautiful plants and flowers, but don't let that fool you—the trainers here are tough. Bring Fire, Flying, or Ice-types to make quick work of their teams.

Gym Trainers

Before you can challenge Erika, you must defeat three Gym Trainers. They all use Grass-types, so bring your best counter.

Gym Leader Erika

Erika uses three Grass-type Pokémon: Victreebel, Tangela, and Vileplume. They are all high-level and have strong Grass moves like Razor Leaf and Sleep Powder. Use Fire or Flying moves to take them down quickly, and be prepared to heal status conditions.

Defeating Erika earns you the Rainbow Badge, which makes all Pokémon up to level 50 obey you, and allows the use of Strength outside of battle. She will also give you TM19 Giga Drain.
