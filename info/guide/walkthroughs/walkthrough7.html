Welcome to Part 7 of the Pokémon Infinite Sun Walkthrough! After dealing with Team Rocket in Celadon City and obtaining the Silph Scope, we now head to Lavender Town to investigate the haunted Pokémon Tower and rescue Mr. Fuji.

Lavender Town

Lavender Town

Lavender Town is a small, quiet town known for its Pokémon Tower, a graveyard for deceased Pokémon. The town has a somber atmosphere, and the locals are currently terrified by ghosts that have appeared in the tower.

Pokémon Tower

The Pokémon Tower is a multi-story building where trainers come to mourn their lost Pokémon. However, Team Rocket has infiltrated the tower, and a powerful ghost is blocking the path to the top. You will need the Silph Scope to identify and battle this ghost.

Tower Trainers

The tower is filled with Channelers, who use Ghost-type Pokémon. Ghost-types are immune to Normal and Fighting moves, so bring a Pokémon with a Dark, Ghost, or Psychic move. You'll also encounter several Team Rocket grunts.

Rival Battle

On the second floor, you'll encounter your rival. He is also here to investigate the tower. He will challenge you to a battle.

The Ghost Marowak

When you reach the 5th floor, a ghost will appear and block your path. If you have the Silph Scope, it will reveal the ghost's true form: a Marowak. This is a special battle—you cannot catch this Marowak, but defeating it will free its spirit and allow you to proceed.

Mr. Fuji

After the ghost is defeated, continue to the top floor of the tower. You'll find Mr. Fuji being held hostage by Team Rocket. Defeat the grunts, and Mr. Fuji will thank you. He will take you back to his house in Lavender Town and give you the Poké Flute, an item that can wake up sleeping Pokémon.
