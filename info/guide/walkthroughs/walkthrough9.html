Welcome to Part 9 of the Pokémon Infinite Sun Walkthrough! After clearing out Silph Co. and defeating Sabrina, we head south to Fuchsia City. Here, you'll explore the famous Safari Zone, find the Warden's Gold Teeth, and obtain HM04 (Strength) before challenging Koga at the Fuchsia Gym.

Fuchsia City

Fuchsia City

Fuchsia City is a large city known for its Safari Zone, a wildlife preserve where trainers can catch rare Pokémon. The city also features a Pokémon Center, a Poké Mart, the Fuchsia Gym, and the Warden's house.

Safari Zone

Safari Zone

The Safari Zone is a special area where you cannot use your own Poké Balls. Instead, you are given 30 Safari Balls and must use Bait or Rocks to catch Pokémon. The zone is divided into four areas, each with different Pokémon. You can also find the Gold Teeth and HM03 Surf here.

Safari Zone Tips

  • Bait makes Pokémon easier to catch but more likely to flee.
  • Rocks make Pokémon harder to catch but less likely to flee.
  • You can find the Gold Teeth in the West Area. Give them to the Warden in Fuchsia City to receive HM04 Strength.
  • In the Secret House in the North Area, you'll find HM03 Surf.

Fuchsia Gym (Koga)

Fuchsia Gym

The Fuchsia Gym specializes in Poison-type Pokémon. The Gym is filled with invisible walls that you must navigate to reach Koga. Poison-types are weak to Ground and Psychic moves, so bring your best counters.

Gym Trainers

Before challenging Koga, you must defeat several Gym Trainers. They use Poison-types like Koffing, Grimer, and Muk.

Gym Leader Koga

Koga uses four Poison-type Pokémon: Koffing, Muk, Koffing, and Weezing. His Pokémon have high Defense and can inflict status conditions like Poison and Sleep. Use Ground or Psychic moves to take them down quickly, and bring Full Heals.

Defeating Koga earns you the Soul Badge, which makes all Pokémon up to level 70 obey you, and allows the use of Surf outside of battle. He will also give you TM06 Toxic.
