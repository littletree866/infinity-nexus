About

Infinite Sun is a ROM hack based on Pokémon FireRed. The goal is to take the original Kanto adventure and expand upon it with more Pokémon, updated mechanics, and significantly more challenging battles.

Almost every major trainer has been redesigned, including Gym Leaders, the Elite Four, and other important battles. Team composition and preparation matter considerably more than they do in the original games.

The adventure also extends beyond the original Kanto experience, with additional content and challenges waiting after the Pokémon League.

Features

Expanded Pokédex

Pokémon from Generations 1–8, with many new options for building your team.

Updated Battle Mechanics

Physical/special split, updated moves, abilities, and various battle improvements.

Mega Evolution

Mega Evolution is available, with selected trainers making use of it as well.

Z-Moves and Dynamax

Additional battle mechanics from later generations are included.

Redesigned Trainers

Gym Leaders, important trainers, and other opponents have new teams and strategies.

Level Caps

Level caps limit overleveling and encourage players to approach battles strategically.

Reusable TMs

TMs can be used repeatedly, making it easier to experiment with different movesets.

Improved Progression

Changes to encounters, trainers, items, and progression throughout Kanto.

Postgame Content

Additional challenges and encounters await beyond the main adventure.
